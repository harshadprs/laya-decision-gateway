Technical Purpose & Architecture

              

                Laya is a 322M parameter multilingual bidirectional encoder built upon the mmBERT foundation. Unlike text-generation models, it computes calibrated categorical scores and probability distributions across 100+ languages in a single forward pass without sampling variance.
